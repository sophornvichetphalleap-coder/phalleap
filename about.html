Welcome to Morakot Khmer Food

Founded in the heart of Phnom Penh, Morakot Khmer Food was born out of a deep love for traditional Cambodian culinary traditions. "Morakot" (មរកត) translates to "Emerald"—symbolizing the richness, purity, and precious nature of our culinary culture.

We take pride in bringing generations-old recipes into a modern dining setting. From our slow-simmered Kroeung pastes to our signature Fish Amok and charcoal-grilled specialties, every dish is crafted with locally sourced fresh ingredients and genuine care.
