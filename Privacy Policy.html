Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

At MORAKOT KHMER FOOD, protecting the privacy of our guests and online visitors is our top priority. This document explains how we handle your personal information when you browse our menu, place orders, or contact us.

📋

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential details to fulfill your food orders and provide a seamless dining experience:

  • Personal Data: Name, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order History: Meal choices, special dietary notes, and delivery times.
  • Technical Data: Basic site usage metrics via standard browser cookies.
⚙️

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information directly powers our restaurant services and customer support:

  • Preparing and delivering your orders accurately and on time.
  • Managing table reservations and customer service requests.
  • Improving our web ordering system and special menu offerings.
🤝

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to marketers. Limited data is shared strictly with trusted operational partners:

  • Local courier and delivery drivers for meal dispatch.
  • Secure payment processors for electronic transactions.
🔒

4. Security & Cookies

We employ encryption and secure protocols to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, keeping your checkout process safe and reliable.
